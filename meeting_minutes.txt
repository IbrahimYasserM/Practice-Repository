Meeting Minutes: Open Source & Contribution Session

1. Open Source Basics
- What is Open Source?
  - Open Source software is publicly available and can be improved and maintained by contributors over time.
  - Some Open Source projects are large, production-ready projects used by millions of people.


2. Why Contribute to Open Source?
- CV & Interviews
  
  - Open Source contributions can add strong real-world experience to your CV.
  - They show that you can work on real projects and deal with large and complex codebases.
  - They also give you real examples to talk about during interviews.

- Learning & Skills
  
  - You learn how to understand and navigate large codebases.
  - You get familiar with professional development workflows and tools used in the industry.

- Networking
  
  - Contributing to Open Source gives you the chance to work and communicate with developers from different countries.

3. Open Source Programs
- Google Summer of Code (GSoC)
  
  - A global program where contributors work on Open Source projects with the help of experienced mentors.
  - It also provides a stipend during the project.

- LFX Mentorship
  
  - A remote mentorship program focused on projects from the Linux Foundation and CNCF.
  - Contributors work directly with maintainers and experienced contributors.

- Other Opportunities
  
  - Igalia and European Summer of Code were also mentioned as other Open Source opportunities.


4. Open Source Mentorship
What the Mentors Will Provide

1. Mentor Support – Guidance and help when working on Open Source projects.
2. Development Guidance – Support through the different stages of contributing to a project.
3. Progress Tracking – Following up on contributors' progress and providing feedback.
