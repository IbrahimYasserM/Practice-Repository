#### <u>First</u>: Google Summer of Code (GSoC) 
>it is a global, online program focused on bringing new contributors into open-source software development

**Program Benefits:**
- Work with open-source organizations under the guidance of experienced mentors

- Gain real-world experience navigating massive codebases and professional workflows

- Receive a stipend to support your development contribution during the project

**Examples of organizations that take-part in GSoC:**
> FreeBSD, MariaDB, The Linux Foundation, Blender
#### <u>Second: </u>Linux Foundation (LFX)
> A global, fully remote program providing structured, paid mentorship across the Linux Foundation and CNCF project ecosystems

**Program Benefits:**
- Work one-on-one with project maintainers and project contributors on defined deliverables
- Gain hands-on production experience in critical infrastructure projects over structured 12-week terms (Spring, Summer, Fall)
- Receive a stipend while building a public contribution record under your own name
